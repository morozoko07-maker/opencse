# Revision sheet — Introduction to Internet of Things

No IA or model paper is included. The bank below comes from the end-of-chapter exercises in the prescribed textbook by Misra, Mukherjee and Roy. No marks are printed.

## Exam pattern

The SEE is 100 marks in three hours, scaled to 50: ten questions, two per module, each of 20 marks with at most three sub-questions; answer five, one per module. CIE is 50, from two 30-mark tests and two 20-mark assignments.

## Module 1 — Basics of networking and the emergence of IoT

### Must know

- Connection types are point-to-point (dedicated link) and point-to-multipoint (shared link).
- Physical topologies: star (central hub, single point of failure), mesh (n(n−1)/2 links, robust but costly), bus (shared backbone) and ring.
- Reach classifies networks as PAN, LAN, MAN and WAN.
- The ISO-OSI model has seven layers: physical, data link, network, transport, session, presentation and application.
- The TCP/IP suite has four layers: network access, internet, transport and application.
- IoT adds sensing, actuation and networked processing; its components include gateways, proxies and border routers.

### Definitions to reproduce

> The Internet of Things is a network of physical things embedded with sensors, software and connectivity that collect and exchange data.

> A network topology is the arrangement of links and nodes in a communication network.

## Module 2 — IoT sensing and actuation

### Must know

- A sensor converts a physical quantity into an electrical signal; a transducer converts one form of energy into another.
- Sensor characteristics: range, resolution, accuracy, precision, sensitivity, linearity, offset, hysteresis and response time.
- Sensors may be scalar or vector, analogue or digital.
- An actuator converts an electrical signal into motion or another physical action.
- Actuator types: hydraulic, pneumatic, electrical, thermal, shape-memory alloy and soft actuators.
- Actuator characteristics include force, stroke, speed, accuracy and power consumption.

### Definitions to reproduce

> A sensor is a device that detects a physical quantity and converts it into a signal that can be measured.

> An actuator is a device that converts an input signal into a physical action or movement.

## Module 3 — IoT processing topologies

### Must know

- IoT data may be structured, semi-structured or unstructured.
- Processing may be on-site (near the device) or off-site (cloud or server).
- Topologies include centralised, distributed and collaborative processing.
- On-site processing reduces latency and bandwidth use; off-site processing offers more storage and computing power.
- Device design: cost, size, power, security. Offloading moves work to a gateway/cloud.

### Definitions to reproduce

> Processing offloading is the transfer of computation from a constrained IoT device to a more capable node.

## Module 4 — Cloud computing and agricultural IoT

### Must know

- Cloud service models: IaaS supplies virtual machines and storage, PaaS supplies a development platform, and SaaS supplies ready applications.
- Deployment models: public, private, hybrid and community.
- Virtualisation divides physical resources into isolated virtual machines.
- An SLA states the expected service level, availability and penalties.
- The sensor-cloud lets many applications share sensors as a service.
- Agricultural IoT uses soil-moisture, humidity, temperature and water-level sensors, cameras, satellites, drones and cloud analytics.

### Definitions to reproduce

> Cloud computing is the on-demand delivery of computing resources over a network, paid for as used.

> A service-level agreement is a contract that defines the level of service a provider must deliver.

## Module 5 — Case studies and future trends

### Must know

- Vehicular IoT connects vehicles, roadside units and cloud services for safety, traffic and diagnostics.
- Healthcare IoT uses body-area sensors, local processing units and cloud storage for monitoring.
- Privacy and security are critical in healthcare because data is personal and sensitive.
- IoT analytics turns sensor data into descriptions, predictions and decisions.
- Machine learning types: supervised, unsupervised and reinforcement learning.

### Definitions to reproduce

> A wireless body area network is a network of wearable or implanted sensors around the human body.

> IoT analytics is the process of collecting and analysing IoT data to support decisions.
