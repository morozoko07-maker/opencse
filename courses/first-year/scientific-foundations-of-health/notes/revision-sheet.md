# Revision sheet — Scientific Foundations of Health

The paper is fifty one-mark MCQs in one hour; revise definitions, numbers and exact WHO and BMI statements. The five modules follow the official syllabus.

## Exam pattern

The syllabus and model paper give 1 hour, 50 marks and 50 compulsory MCQs with no negative marking. CIE is 50 marks from two 40-mark IAs and assignments. The pass mark is 40% in CIE and 35% in SEE, with 40% overall.

## Module 1 — Good health and a positive mindset

### Must know

- WHO health: a state of complete physical, mental and social well-being, not merely the absence of disease or infirmity.
- Wellness is the active process of growth and change towards the fullest health and well-being. Health is the goal; wellness is the process.
- Dimensions of wellness: physical, intellectual, emotional, social, spiritual, occupational and environmental. The model paper counts six.
- Psychological disorders include anxiety, depression and psychosis. Psychosis is marked by loss of touch with reality.

### Definitions to reproduce

> Health is a state of complete physical, mental and social well-being, and not merely the absence of disease or infirmity.

> Wellness is the active process of growth and change to reach the fullest health and well-being.

## Module 2 — Healthy lifestyles

### Must know

- A balanced diet supplies carbohydrates, proteins, fats, vitamins, minerals, fibre and water in the right proportions.
- Carbohydrates are the main energy source; proteins build and repair tissue; fats store energy; vitamins and minerals regulate body functions.
- BMI = weight in kg / (height in m)². Overweight is BMI > 25; obesity is BMI > 30.
- Eating disorders: anorexia nervosa (15% below normal weight, BMI ≤ 17.5), bulimia nervosa (binge and purge) and binge-eating disorder.
- Fitness components: cardiorespiratory endurance, muscular strength, muscular endurance, flexibility and body composition.

### Definitions to reproduce

> Body mass index is a person's weight in kilograms divided by the square of height in metres.

> Obesity is excess body fat, commonly defined as a BMI greater than 30.

## Module 3 — Healthy and caring relationships

### Must know

- Communication needs listening as well as speaking. A good listener keeps eye contact, does not interrupt and checks understanding by restating.
- Friendship may be dyadic (two people) or egalitarian (equal standing). Healthy friendships are supportive and allow honesty.
- Signs of a toxic friendship include lack of empathy, constant criticism, jealousy, manipulation and pressure.
- Relationships affect health through support, stress and belonging. Isolation and conflict raise stress and reduce well-being.

### Definitions to reproduce

> Communication is the exchange of information, ideas and feelings through a common system of symbols.

## Module 4 — Avoiding risks and harmful habits

### Must know

- Health-compromising behaviours include smoking, alcohol misuse, drug abuse, unsafe sex, poor diet and inactivity.
- Addiction has four features: negative consequences, loss of control, habitual behaviour and denial.
- Addiction develops through experimentation, regular use, dependence and withdrawal. Types include substance and behavioural addictions.
- Neurotransmitters are chemicals that relay impulses from one nerve cell to another; dopamine is central to reward.

### Definitions to reproduce

> Addiction is a compulsive need to use a substance or repeat a behaviour despite harmful consequences.

> A neurotransmitter is a chemical that carries a signal from one nerve cell to another.

## Module 5 — Preventing and fighting disease

### Must know

- Infection spreads by contact, droplets, air, contaminated food or water, vectors and blood.
- Prevention: hand hygiene, safe water, vaccination, safe food handling, vector control and avoiding needle sharing.
- Immunity may be innate or acquired; vaccines produce acquired immunity without causing the disease.
- Chronic conditions such as diabetes, hypertension and heart disease need long-term management, medication and lifestyle change.

### Definitions to reproduce

> Immunity is the body's ability to resist infection through defence mechanisms and antibodies.

## Last 24 hours

1. Reproduce the WHO definition and the BMI formula exactly.
2. Learn the BMI cut-offs, anorexia criteria and four features of addiction.
3. List the dimensions of wellness and the components of fitness.
4. Learn the modes of disease transmission and the prevention measures.
